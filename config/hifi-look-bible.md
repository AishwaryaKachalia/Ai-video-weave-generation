# Hi-Fi — Layer Zero: Look Bible (LOCKED — do not vary between shots)

## Product (must appear in every frame)
- `products/layer-zero/can.png` — 500ml tall can, matte deep navy, cream block "HI / FI" lettering, gold "HIFI BEER CO." above, white "ICE COLD BEER" at top, gold "STRONG LAGER" at bottom, fine condensation droplets, silver top with pull tab.
- Never alter: can shape, colors, lettering, layout, or text. Label must be legible and correctly spelled whenever facing camera. Attach can.png as reference on every generation.

## Photography style (refs: `style-refs/hifi/ref-1..4.webp`)
- Real 35mm analog film, candid and editorial: Kodak Portra / Cinestill-like stock, shot on a 35mm compact or SLR with on-camera/direct flash blended with warm practical light.
- Documentary, lived-in nightlife moments: friends at bars, dance floors, card tables, lounges. Natural unposed expressions, hands in frame, overhead and over-the-shoulder angles, foreground elements (hands, hair, shoulders) softly out of focus.
- Shallow depth of field, soft organic falloff, gentle halation/glow around lights, fine natural film grain.
- Premium and clean: tasteful styling, rich warm wood, leather, brass, glassware, jewelry. Not messy, not cheap.

## Color grade (fixed across all shots)
- Warm amber/golden highlights and skin tones; deep warm-brown to soft green-black shadows with lifted, never crushed, blacks.
- Rich but muted saturation; amber drinks glow, navy of the can reads true.
- SOFT contrast, gentle tonal rolloff, creamy highlights. No HDR, no crushed blacks, no blown whites, no teal-orange blockbuster look.

## Avoid
High-contrast/harsh look, over-sharpened or plastic AI skin, CGI gloss, oversaturation, stock-photo posing, warped hands/faces, extra fingers, altered or misspelled can text, other beer brands.

The Hi-Fi 'Layer Zero' navy can, exactly as in the reference image, unaltered label and text, is clearly present in frame."

## HARD RULES (added after rejected v1/v2 — user feedback)
- Cans are NEVER hero-posed or held up to camera. They are incidental: in hands at waist/chest, on tables, ledges, ground — mid-frame or background, label partly or fully visible, like a real candid photo. Camera focuses on people and moments, not the product.
- Match the REFERENCE photos' feel: candid, imperfect framing, direct flash on faces, warm dark falloff, visible grain, slight blur/motion, people not looking at camera.
- Setting must be a REALISTIC Mumbai terrace house party: low/mid-rise concrete apartment buildings, water tanks, AC units, hazy warm dusk sky, cheap string lights/bulbs hung unevenly, plastic/cane chairs, speaker, folding table with snacks, ice bucket. NO glossy glass skyline, no stock-photo rooftop bar.
- Real, ordinary, attractive young Indian people in everyday party clothes (kurtas, tees, shirts, jeans, slip dresses), natural uneven skin, no airbrushed models.
- Never put the product name in prose where the model could print it. Refer to "the exact can in image 1". Never invent can text.
- Horizontal 16:9 unless told otherwise. Sunset-to-night timing only.
- Show every generated frame to the user in chat. Always get approval before spending credits.
