# Hi-Fi — Layer Zero: Look Bible (LOCKED — do not vary between shots)

## Product (must appear in every frame)
- `products/layer-zero/can.png` — 500ml tall can, matte deep navy, cream block "HI / FI" lettering, gold "HIFI BEER CO." above, white "ICE COLD BEER" at top, gold "STRONG LAGER" at bottom, fine condensation droplets, silver top with pull tab.
- Never alter: can shape, colors, lettering, layout, or text. Label must be legible and correctly spelled whenever facing camera. Attach can.png as reference on every generation.

## Photography style (refs: `style-refs/hifi/ref-1..4.webp`)
- Real 35mm analog film, candid and editorial: Kodak Portra / Cinestill-like stock, shot on a 35mm compact or SLR with on-camera/direct flash blended with warm practical light.
- Documentary, lived-in nightlife moments: friends at bars, dance floors, card tables, lounges. Natural unposed expressions, hands in frame, overhead and over-the-shoulder angles, foreground elements (hands, hair, shoulders) softly out of focus.
- Shallow depth of field, soft organic falloff, gentle halation/glow around lights, fine natural film grain.
- Premium and clean: tasteful styling, rich warm wood, leather, brass, glassware, jewelry. Not messy, not cheap.

## Color grade (fixed across all shots)
- Warm amber/golden highlights and skin tones; deep warm-brown to soft green-black shadows with lifted, never crushed, blacks.
- Rich but muted saturation; amber drinks glow, navy of the can reads true.
- SOFT contrast, gentle tonal rolloff, creamy highlights. No HDR, no crushed blacks, no blown whites, no teal-orange blockbuster look.

## Avoid
High-contrast/harsh look, over-sharpened or plastic AI skin, CGI gloss, oversaturation, stock-photo posing, warped hands/faces, extra fingers, altered or misspelled can text, other beer brands.

## Prompt prefix (prepend to every shot)
"Candid 35mm analog film photograph, Kodak Portra-style warm amber grade, soft contrast with lifted blacks, subtle direct-flash blended with warm practical light, shallow depth of field, fine natural film grain, soft halation, premium editorial nightlife, natural skin texture. The Hi-Fi 'Layer Zero' navy can, exactly as in the reference image, unaltered label and text, is clearly present in frame."

## HARD RULES (added after rejected v1/v2 — user feedback)
- Cans are NEVER hero-posed or held up to camera. They are incidental: in hands at waist/chest, on tables, ledges, ground — mid-frame or background, label partly or fully visible, like a real candid photo. Camera focuses on people and moments, not the product.
- Match the REFERENCE photos' feel: candid, imperfect framing, direct flash on faces, warm dark falloff, visible grain, slight blur/motion, people not looking at camera.
- Setting: PREMIUM upper-middle-class Mumbai (Bandra/Worli/Juhu vibe): a clean, stylish apartment terrace or rooftop lounge at NIGHT — wooden deck, lounge seating, warm lanterns/string lights, plants, sleek high-rise or sea-facing building lights blurred far behind. NO slums, tin roofs, water tanks, cracked concrete, plastic chairs, or fake glossy CGI skyline.
- Time: NIGHT (dark sky) for party frames. Skate frame stays sunset.
- Primary framing ref: style-refs/hifi/night-dance-ref.webp — tight, close, flash-lit dancing/laughing faces, hands and drinks in frame, dark background, strong flash falloff.
- CAN ACCURACY: cans must be exact (silver top, navy body, cream HI/FI, gold HIFI BEER CO. + STRONG LAGER, white ICE COLD BEER). Keep cans large enough in frame to render text correctly (hand-held, 1/5 of frame height or more); if text is wrong, composite the real can art in post rather than regenerating.
- Real, ordinary, attractive young Indian people in everyday party clothes (kurtas, tees, shirts, jeans, slip dresses), natural uneven skin, no airbrushed models.
- Never put the product name in prose where the model could print it. Refer to "the exact can in image 1". Never invent can text.
- Horizontal 16:9 unless told otherwise. Sunset-to-night timing only.
- Show every generated frame to the user in chat. Always get approval before spending credits.
- FLASH must look real: hard direct on-camera flash, bright punchy subjects with specular skin highlights, hard-edged shadow cast on the wall/ground behind them, background falls to near-black with only dim blurred ambient lights. Never evenly lit, never "warm lamp-lit" pretending to be flash.
- FASHION must be Gen Z (24-34 per brief): baby tees, oversized boxy tees, baggy/low-rise/ripped denim, cargos, wallet chains, beaded and layered necklaces, bandanas, beanies, tinted sunglasses, platform and chunky sneakers, tattoos. No real brand logos or patches anywhere.

## VIDEO RULES (added after rooftop clip face distortion)
- Faces must never distort. Before showing ANY clip, inspect a 4fps contact sheet of the whole clip, crop to every visible face, and trim to the last clean frame. Never show a clip with a melting, smeared or blank face.
- Generate 3s clips (matching storyboard slots), not 5s: drift and face breakdown happen after ~2.5s. Fewer people in frame, and no hands or arms crossing faces in the source still.
- Prompt every video: "faces stay stable and unchanged, no hand or arm passes in front of any face, no new people enter the frame".
- No zoom, push-in, slow motion or flash/lens flare unless the storyboard asks. Natural handheld, real-time motion, warm grade locked.

## DELIVERABLES (per storyboard + user)
- Every approved frame needs BOTH a 16:9 and a 9:16 version, and every video is generated twice (16:9 investor master, 9:16 mobile/WhatsApp). Make the 9:16 still first (re-framed from the approved 16:9 still, same people/can/look), then animate each from its own still. Never centre-crop a 16:9 still into 9:16.
