# Reference Video Shot Breakdown — SoBo Wrap Trouser

Source: `reference/wrap-trouser-beige-sobo-reference.mp4` (720x1280, 30fps, 13.3s).
Contact sheet: `reference/wrap-trouser-beige-sobo-contact-sheet.jpg` (2fps frame grid).

The reference is an NYC street-style reel that changes outfit at each of 4
locations, wiped between by a yellow NYC cab whipping past camera. This video
adapts it to **one product** (Wrap Trouser, beige) and **one location family**
(South Bombay) — she keeps the same outfit throughout, and the taxi wipe
becomes a black-and-yellow "kaali peeli" Mumbai taxi.

| # | Time range | Reference content | SoBo adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–4.5 | On the phone, hand in hair, building awning + parked scooter behind, at a crosswalk | Same pose/energy, SoBo colonial street corner, parked Vespa/Chetak-style scooter, zebra crossing |
| — | 4.5–5.0 | Yellow NYC cab whips past camera (wipe) | **Kaali peeli** taxi whips past camera (wipe) |
| 2 | 5.0–7.0 | Hand brushing hair back, roadside flower stall behind her | Same pose, generic SoBo roadside flower-seller stall (no signage) |
| — | 7.0–7.5 | Yellow cab wipe | Kaali peeli wipe |
| 3 | 7.5–10.0 | Standing by a red fire hydrant, tree-lined street, parked cars | Standing by a red colonial-style pillar postbox, tree-lined SoBo street, parked cars |
| — | 10.0–10.5 | Yellow cab wipe | Kaali peeli wipe |
| 4 | 10.5–13.3 | Triumphant arm-raise under a grand stone archway with a hanging lamp | Same pose under a SoBo colonial stone archway (Gothic/Indo-Saracenic detailing), wrought-iron hanging lamp |

## Style notes

- Grade: bright, high-key, saturated — candid daylight influencer content, not moody/desaturated.
- Depth of field: mostly medium-deep, full body/medium compositions in frame; not the extreme-shallow macro style of the Flap Trouser campaign.
- Movement: mostly static or very light handheld sway — no walking motion blur within a segment; all the "motion energy" is concentrated in the taxi wipes between segments.
- Continuity: same model, same outfit (Wrap Trouser beige) across all 4 segments — this is a single-product showcase, not an outfit-change reel like the source.
- Taxi wipes are their own short full-frame insert clips (no model in frame), not a crossfade filter — matches how `scripts/stitch.py` already supports per-shot clips with `transition_in: "cut"`.
