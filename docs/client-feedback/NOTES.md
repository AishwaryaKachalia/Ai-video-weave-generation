# Client feedback on the 20s teaser (received 2026-11-04)

Source screenshots: 01–04 in this folder.

## What the client said
- "The 'big' message of the opportunity is not coming out." The market number is buried (we show it at 13–15.6s for ~1s each, over a dimmed crowd).
- Themes, in order: 1) BIG MARKET  2) NEW GENERATION  3) BRAND AMBITION. The first few seconds must be a strong hook.
- Revised storyboard: 0–2 THE WORLD'S NEXT BIG BEER STORY / 2–4 IS BEING WRITTEN IN INDIA / 4–6 $23 BILLION BY 2035 (large orange numbers on navy) / 6–9 MEET HIFI (can reveal, bass drop) / 9–13 A NEW GENERATION. A NEW BEER. (fast montage) / 13–16 BUILT FOR THE HIGHS (broader world) / 16–20 hero + logo + INDIA'S NEXT BIG BEER STORY.
- Key feedback: stronger opening (no slow intro); broader consumer world (we lean on one house-party setting: add street culture, fashion, rooftops, music, different social settings, a generation not one group of friends); more dramatic product reveal synced to the drop, keep navy-and-white pack; more attitude and authenticity (candid, 24–34, no staged ad interactions); stronger sense of scale (modern India → consumer → product → national ambition); less text, bold oversized animated type that lands fast.

## Reference ads they liked
- Nike, "Nothing Beats a Londoner" (youtube 26qmJzTCRG4): letterboxed, real locals in real local spaces (barbershop, boxing ring, estate at night), candid expressive faces.
- Nike, "You Can't Stop Us" 2020 (youtube pcXTnyCmQbg): split-screen match cuts joining two different people/places mid-gesture.
